We are looking for individuals who are passionate about creating new products, features, and services from scratch while managing ambiguity and the pace of a company where development cycles are measured in weeks, not years.

About the team
AWS Serverless Compute is changing the way customers build in the cloud — enabling scalable, fault-tolerant applications without managing infrastructure. Amazon Elastic Container Service (ECS) is AWS's fully managed container orchestration service within this portfolio, running millions of containers daily across Fargate, EC2, and Managed Instances, solving hard problems in distributed systems, security, and performance at massive scale.
